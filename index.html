Featured Project

The Politics of Pilgrimage

Hajj Quota Allocations and Muslim Population Rates Across the Middle East · GEOG 395 Cartography and Geovisualization · Spring 2026

Saudi Arabia controls how many pilgrims each country may send to Hajj through a quota of one slot per 1,000 Muslim residents. I designed a bivariate thematic map in ArcGIS Pro comparing absolute Hajj quota and quota as a rate of Muslim population across 10 Middle Eastern countries, then built a flow map and an attendance chart and assembled them into an ArcGIS StoryMap.

Overall the map reveals that Hajj quota allocation is not a straightforward reflection of Muslim population size.
